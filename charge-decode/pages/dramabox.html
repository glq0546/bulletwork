What is DRAMABOX on my credit card statement?

Quick Answer: A charge from DRAMABOX is a short-drama streaming app that bills for virtual coin packs or weekly VIP subscriptions. Charges often come from family members unlocking episodes, and deleting the app does not stop the billing. To stop charges, cancel through your device's subscription settings (Apple, Google Play, etc.). Refunds are available through the app store's refund portal for unauthorized in-app purchases or accidental charges.

1. What is DRAMABOX?

Dramabox is an app designed for watching short, vertical-format soap operas. Instead of a single monthly fee, the app often charges you to buy digital coins to unlock the next episode of a cliffhanger. It also offers auto-renewing weekly or monthly VIP memberships. Depending on whether you downloaded the app on an iPhone or an Android device, the bill might show up directly as DRAMABOX, or it might be bundled under Apple, Google Play, or PayPal.

Merchant Code: DRAMABOX

Billing Pattern: In-app coin purchases or auto-renewing streaming subscription

Recommended action: Check your phone's purchase history and verify if a family member has been watching micro-dramas.

2. Why are you seeing this charge?

Unmanaged family device purchases

A child or family member tapped 'next episode' and repeatedly bought digital coin bundles to keep watching.

Background subscription renewals

You or someone else signed up for a weekly VIP pass, and it auto-renewed multiple times before you spotted it.

Promotional pricing rollover

You signed up for a cheap introductory trial or discounted plan, which quietly rolled over into a much higher standard rate.

Rapid microtransactions

You see a long string of small, rapid charges that look like bank errors, but are actually separate point-of-sale unlocks.

App deletion misunderstandings

You uninstalled the app from your device, assuming that would end the billing, but the background subscription stayed active.

3. Is This a Scam or Legit?

⚠️ Watch out for aggressive microtransactions.

The app itself is real and available on official app stores, but its payment design can feel highly deceptive. The weekly subscription fees are incredibly high compared to traditional streaming services, and constant prompts to unlock clips make accidental purchases very common. If you do not watch these dramas and don't share your device with anyone, treat the charge as unauthorized and act quickly.

4. How to identify the account

If you have an iPhone or iPad, review your Apple App Store purchase history and active subscriptions.
If you use an Android, check the 'Payments & subscriptions' menu inside the Google Play Store app.
If you have the app installed, open it to check the profile page, but be careful not to trigger any automatic one-tap purchases.

6. How to stop future charges

Cancel through your device's subscription portal. If you signed up via Apple or Google, you must cancel inside your phone's native settings. Simply deleting the app or emailing the developer will not stop the charges.
Document your cancellation. Take screenshots of the confirmation screen and save any email receipts showing the auto-renewal has been disabled.
Secure your account details. If you continue to see charges after canceling, ask your credit card company to place a merchant block on the descriptor to prevent further attempts.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Dramabox keep charging me?

You might have an active weekly or monthly VIP subscription, or someone in your household could be buying coin packs to unlock episodes. Remember, simply deleting the app from your phone won't stop the automatic billing.

Can Apple or Google refund Dramabox purchases?

Yes, both Apple and Google regularly approve refunds for accidental purchases or charges made by children, as long as you request them quickly through their official refund portals.

How do I prevent future charges?

Go to your phone's subscription settings to cancel active plans, turn off one-tap buying, and set up your device to require password confirmation for every in-app purchase.
