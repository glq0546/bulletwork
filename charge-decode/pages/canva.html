What is CANVA on my credit card statement?

A charge from CANVA on your bank statement is almost always connected to their graphic design platform. This usually happens when a Canva Pro or Canva Teams subscription automatically renews, a free trial runs out, or someone in your home or office orders physical prints or custom merchandise.

1. What is CANVA?

CANVA represents the global graphic design platform Canva Pty Ltd. Because bank statement space is so limited, you won't see the specific name of your design project or team workspace on your bill—just a simple 'CANVA' line item.

Merchant descriptor: CANVA

Billing pattern: Canva Pro or Teams renewals, physical print orders, premium template purchases, or a trial rollover.

Recommended action: Check your personal and work emails for a Canva invoice, or check if anyone in your home or company recently created designs.

2. Why are you seeing this charge?

A Canva Pro auto-renewal: Your annual or monthly Pro membership renewed automatically. This often surprises users who signed up for a trial and forgot to cancel it.
An unexpected 'Teams' seat charge: Canva's team billing structure charges per person. If you invited a coworker, client, or family member to collaborate, you might have been billed for extra seats without realizing it.
A physical print or merchandise order: You or someone in your household ordered physical prints, business cards, posters, or custom apparel directly through the Canva Print service.
One-time premium purchases: You bought a specific premium template, stock photo, or font to complete a single project.
The wrong account was canceled: You might have canceled a personal Canva account while a second profile—like a work or school email—remained active and kept billing you.

3. Is this a scam or legit?

⚠️ Legitimate platform, but watch out for accidental upgrades.

While Canva is an established and highly trusted service, many users find themselves billed unexpectedly due to its seat-sharing model or automatic trial rollovers. However, if absolutely nobody in your household or business uses Canva and you cannot locate an account, your card details may have been used fraudulently.

4. How to trace the charge back to an account

Search your email history: Look through all your active email accounts for terms like 'Canva', 'invoice', 'pro', 'subscription', or the exact charge amount.
Try logging in with different emails: Sign in to Canva using any personal, work, school, or secondary emails you own to see which one holds the active plan.
Review your workspace settings: If you are part of a team, check the billing tab in your Canva dashboard to see if you were billed for extra team members or an upgraded workspace.

6. How to stop future charges

Cancel inside your billing settings: Log into the correct Canva profile, go to Account Settings, select Billing & Plans, and click cancel. Simply deleting the mobile app from your phone will not stop the charges.
Remove unneeded team members: If you are keeping Canva but want to lower your bill, make sure to remove any extra seats or inactive users from your workspace before your next billing cycle.
Back up your files first: Before downgrading or closing a team workspace, make sure to download any important projects or assets so you don't lose access to them.

7. How to get your money back

Step 1. Find out exactly which email address or team workspace is linked to the credit card bill.
Step 2. Cancel the subscription immediately to prevent another auto-renewal while your refund is being processed.
Step 3. Reach out to Canva's official customer support, providing them with your invoice number, billing date, and account email.
Step 4. Pull together any supporting details, such as screenshots showing duplicate profiles or accidental member invites.
Step 5. Contact your bank to file a dispute if Canva is unable to locate the transaction or refuses to refund an unauthorized charge.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Is CANVA a scam?

No, Canva is a highly popular graphic design platform. However, if you see a charge you didn't expect, you should verify if a family member used your card, if an old trial rolled over, or if you were billed for extra team seats.

How do I stop future charges?

You can stop future billing by logging into your Canva account, heading to your billing settings, and canceling the active subscription. Be sure to save the cancellation email for your records.

When should I call my bank?

Reach out to your card issuer immediately if you have confirmed that nobody in your home or office authorized the charge, or if Canva continues to bill you after you canceled your account.
