What is GODADDY.COM on my credit card statement?

A charge from GODADDY.COM is for a domain, website hosting, or another online service through GoDaddy. You probably bought a domain a few years ago and forgot about the auto-renewal.

Full Name & What It Is

GODADDY.COM stands for GoDaddy. They are one of the world's biggest domain registrars and web hosting companies. You see this charge when you buy stuff from them. Common products include domain registrations, web hosting, SSL certificates, and Microsoft 365 email.

Prices look cheap at first. You might pay $0.99 to $19.99 for your first year of a domain. When it renews, the price jumps to $25 to $80 a year. Web hosting runs $5.99 to $24.99 a month. SSL certificates cost $63.99 to $149.99 a year.

GoDaddy turns on auto-renewal by default. That cheap first-year deal turns into a huge bill later. They also add "domain privacy protection" as a separate auto-renewing charge. It is easy to miss this extra fee.

Merchant Code: GODADDY.COM

Company: GoDaddy Inc.

Service: Domain Registration, Web Hosting, SSL Certificate, or Email Subscription

Common Scenarios

1. Expired Domain Renewal

You grabbed a $0.99 domain for a side project two years ago, forgot about it, and just got hit with a $40 renewal.

2. Web Hosting Auto-Renew

Your web hosting plan renewed automatically at the standard monthly rate.

3. SSL Certificate Renewal

An SSL certificate expired and auto-renewed at full price.

4. Former Employee's Purchase

A former employee bought a domain for your business on your company card, and it's still renewing.

5. Domain Privacy Fee

You got charged for both your domain and a separate "domain privacy" fee.

Is This a Scam?

⚠️ Legitimate, but auto-renewal feels sneaky.

Honestly, GoDaddy is a real company, not a scam. But their auto-renewal policy makes people feel tricked. You agree to a cheap price, then get billed way more later without a clear reminder.

Don't panic yet. Go to your GoDaddy account and check your product list. Look at the billing history. You will likely spot the exact service that charged you.

How to Get a Refund

Step 1. Log into your account at godaddy.com/account.
Step 2. Check your product list and billing history. Find the charge that matches your credit card statement.
Step 3. Cancel the auto-renewal for that product. Then, request a refund for the recent charge.
Step 4. If GoDaddy won't refund you, call customer support at 1-480-505-8877. You can also call your bank to dispute the charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did GODADDY.COM charge my credit card?

GoDaddy likely charged you for an auto-renewed domain, web hosting, or SSL certificate. They often offer cheap first-year prices that renew at much higher rates later.

How do I get a refund from GoDaddy?

Log into your account at godaddy.com/account, cancel the auto-renewal, and request a refund. If it is within 30 days of the charge, calling 1-480-505-8877 usually works.

Does GoDaddy charge for domain privacy separately?

Yes, GoDaddy often adds "domain privacy protection" as a separate auto-renewing charge on top of your domain registration fee.
