Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our services:

Information You Provide

  • Search queries when you search for a merchant
  • Error reports and feedback you submit through our forms
  • Email address if you contact us directly

Information Collected Automatically

  • Pages visited and time spent (via analytics)
  • Referrer URL (how you found our site)
  • Browser type and device information
  • IP address (anonymized for analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve our merchant lookup service
  • Understand which merchants are most searched for prioritization
  • Respond to your inquiries and error reports
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our platform

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for site functionality and privacy-respecting analytics. We do NOT use invasive advertising trackers.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Our core merchant search functionality works without cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Vercel: Hosting and CDN (processes request logs)
  • Cloudflare: DNS and security (processes traffic data)
  • Analytics: Privacy-focused, anonymized usage statistics

Each third-party service has their own privacy policy governing their data handling.

5. Data Retention

We retain search logs and analytics data for a maximum of 90 days. Error reports and feedback may be retained indefinitely to improve our service. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@glq-api.asia.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. For significant changes, we will provide additional notice.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: privacy@glq-api.asia
